Questions

What exactly is measured here?

Ethereum's EIP-1559 base fee, in gwei, read directly from block headers via public JSON-RPC nodes. Roughly 670 blocks are sampled across the last seven days and averaged into each hour-of-day / day-of-week cell.

Why does gas get expensive at certain hours?

Base fee rises automatically when blocks get full. Activity clusters around US and European working hours, so late UTC morning through early afternoon tends to be the most expensive stretch. Weekends are usually quieter.

Does this work for L2s?

No. This tracks Ethereum mainnet only. Rollups like Base, Arbitrum and Optimism have their own fee markets, usually far cheaper, and are not reflected here.

Is the data live?

It refreshes on a schedule and the timestamp above the heatmap shows the last successful update. Nothing here is a price prediction — it is a summary of what already happened on-chain.
